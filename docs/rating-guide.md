# Rating guide

Thank you for helping. You will score translations from English into Cebuano. Each row shows an English sentence and one translation. You will not know which system wrote it, and the same sentence appears more than once with different translations.

Give each row two whole-number scores from 1 to 5. Score what is on the page, and don't try to guess who wrote it.

## Adequacy: how much of the meaning survived?

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | All of the meaning is there |
| 4 | Most of it, with small details lost or added |
| 3 | The main point is there, but something important is lost or wrong |
| 2 | Only fragments of the meaning are there |
| 1 | None of it, or a different sentence altogether |

## Fluency: does it sound like natural Cebuano?

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | A native speaker could have written it |
| 4 | Natural, with one or two awkward spots |
| 3 | Understandable, but clearly not natural |
| 2 | Hard to follow |
| 1 | Not understandable as Cebuano |

Judge the two scores separately. A sentence can sound perfectly natural and still say the wrong thing.

Some rows are checks to confirm the sheet is working, so you may see a translation that doesn't match its sentence. Score those the same way as any other row. Scoring goes faster if you don't overthink it, and your first reaction is usually right. If you leave a row blank, it's simply skipped, but please fill in both scores or neither.
