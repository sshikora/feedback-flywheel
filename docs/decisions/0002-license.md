# 0002: Apache 2.0

**Status:** Accepted, 2026-10-31

**Context.** The choice was between MIT and Apache 2.0. Both are permissive.

**Decision.** Apache 2.0. Its explicit patent grant is what company legal teams look for before adopting a tool, and it is the norm for machine learning infrastructure.

**Consequences.** The license covers code only. Datasets keep their own terms.
